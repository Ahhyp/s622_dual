## M2.8.3 pick_handover_place.xml

### 流程设计

按 M2.8.1 决定:**左臂 pick → 双臂到 handover_ready → 交接 → 右臂 place**。

关键设计点:

1. **交接时序** — 严格顺序,不能并发:

   ```
   right close → transfer (atomic) → left open
   ```

   * `right close` 咬住 cube(cube 此刻同时被两个夹爪夹持)
   * `transfer` 原子 detach(left) + attach(right)
   * `left open` 松开(此时 cube 只被 right 夹持,不掉)

2. **交接前后位姿**:

   * Pick 完成后左臂到 `safe` (M2.7 单臂 safe pose,left_arm 独立走)
   * `DualMoveToJointState → dual_handover_ready` 双臂同步到位
   * 交接完成后 `DualMoveToJointState → dual_home` 双臂回位
   * 然后**右臂**独立走 place (复用 M2.7 后半段)

3. **可能的坑**:

   * Left 独立走 safe 时 right 可能挡路 → 用 `dual_home` 保证起点干净
   * Right pick 完 place 后要回位,继续用 `dual_home`

### XML 文件

新建 `s622_bt_manager/behavior_trees/pick_handover_place.xml`:

```xml
<root BTCPP_format="4">
  <BehaviorTree ID="PickHandoverPlace">
    <Fallback name="MainWithRecovery">

      <!-- ============ 主任务 ============ -->
      <Sequence name="MainTask">
        <CheckSystemReady />

        <!-- ============ 起点: 双臂到 home ============ -->
        <DualMoveToJointState named_pose="dual_home"
            velocity_scale="0.3" acceleration_scale="0.3"
            timeout_sec="20.0" />

        <!-- ============ 左臂 Pick ============ -->
        <SetGripper arm_prefix="left" command="open" timeout_sec="5.0" />

        <RetryUntilSuccessful num_attempts="5">
          <Sequence>
            <DetectObject object_name="cube" min_confidence="0.10" max_age_sec="1.0"
                output_u="{u}" output_v="{v}"
                output_yaw="{obj_yaw}" output_confidence="{conf}" />
            <GenerateGraspCandidate arm_prefix="left"
                u="{u}" v="{v}" object_yaw="{obj_yaw}"
                tcp_grasp_pose="{grasp_pose}"
                tcp_pregrasp_pose="{pregrasp_pose}"
                grasp_yaw="{grasp_yaw}"
                grasp_pose_x="{grasp_x}"
                grasp_pose_y="{grasp_y}" />
          </Sequence>
        </RetryUntilSuccessful>

        <LockTargetPixel input_u="{u}" input_v="{v}"
            locked_u="{lu}" locked_v="{lv}" />

        <MoveToPose arm_prefix="left" target_pose="{pregrasp_pose}"
            velocity_scale="0.2" acceleration_scale="0.2"
            ensure_servo_stopped="true"
            timeout_sec="25.0" />

        <VisualAlign arm_prefix="left" mode="xy"
            target_x_base="{grasp_x}" target_y_base="{grasp_y}"
            tolerance_m="0.005"
            ensure_servo_started="true"
            timeout_sec="25.0" />

        <VisualAlign arm_prefix="left" mode="yaw"
            target_yaw="{grasp_yaw}"
            tolerance_rad="0.10"
            ensure_servo_started="true"
            timeout_sec="5.0" />

        <AlignLockCheck locked_u="{lu}" locked_v="{lv}"
            object_name="cube"
            min_confidence="0.05"
            tolerance_px="25.0" />

        <VisualAlign arm_prefix="left" mode="descend"
            distance="0.13"
            speed="0.04"
            ensure_servo_started="true"
            timeout_sec="30.0" />

        <SetGripper arm_prefix="left" command="close"
            timeout_sec="5.0" finger_position="{finger_pos}" />

        <VerifyGrasp arm_prefix="left"
            finger_min_position="0.005" timeout_sec="5.0"
            finger_position="{finger_pos}" />

        <AttachObject arm_prefix="left" object_name="cube"
            size_x="0.04" size_y="0.04" size_z="0.04"
            offset_z="0.02" />

        <VisualAlign arm_prefix="left" mode="lift"
            distance="0.10" speed="0.05"
            ensure_servo_started="false"
            timeout_sec="20.0" />

        <!-- ============ 双臂到 handover 位姿 ============ -->
        <DualMoveToJointState named_pose="dual_handover_ready"
            velocity_scale="0.15" acceleration_scale="0.15"
            timeout_sec="20.0" />

        <!-- ============ 交接: 严格顺序 ============ -->
        <!-- 1. 右手咬 (cube 此刻被双夹爪同时夹持) -->
        <SetGripper arm_prefix="right" command="close" timeout_sec="5.0" />

        <!-- 2. Planning scene 原子转移: left_grasp_frame -> right_grasp_frame -->
        <TransferObject object_name="cube"
            new_arm_prefix="right"
            offset_z="0.02"
            timeout_sec="3.0" />

        <!-- 3. 左手松 (cube 只被右手夹持) -->
        <SetGripper arm_prefix="left" command="open" timeout_sec="5.0" />

        <!-- ============ 双臂回 home (左臂让路给右臂 place) ============ -->
        <DualMoveToJointState named_pose="dual_home"
            velocity_scale="0.2" acceleration_scale="0.2"
            timeout_sec="20.0" />

        <!-- ============ 右臂 Place ============ -->
        <GeneratePlaceCandidate arm_prefix="right"
            place_pose="{place_pose}"
            pre_place_pose="{pre_place_pose}" />

        <MoveToPose arm_prefix="right" target_pose="{pre_place_pose}"
            velocity_scale="0.20" acceleration_scale="0.20"
            timeout_sec="20.0"
            ensure_servo_stopped="true" />

        <MoveToPose arm_prefix="right" target_pose="{place_pose}"
            velocity_scale="0.10" acceleration_scale="0.10"
            timeout_sec="15.0"
            ensure_servo_stopped="true" />

        <SetGripper arm_prefix="right" command="open" timeout_sec="5.0" />

        <DetachObject arm_prefix="right" object_name="cube"
            put_back_in_world="true"
            drop_pose="{place_pose}" />

        <VisualAlign arm_prefix="right" mode="lift"
            distance="0.10" speed="0.05"
            timeout_sec="20.0" />

        <!-- ============ 收尾: 双臂回 home ============ -->
        <DualMoveToJointState named_pose="dual_home"
            velocity_scale="0.3" acceleration_scale="0.3"
            timeout_sec="20.0" />
      </Sequence>

      <!-- ============ Recovery ============ -->
      <Sequence name="Recovery">
        <PrintMessage msg="=== ENTERING RECOVERY ==="/>

        <!-- Stop 两臂的 servo (可能有一个在跑) -->
        <ForceSuccess>
          <StopServo arm_prefix="left" />
        </ForceSuccess>
        <ForceSuccess>
          <StopServo arm_prefix="right" />
        </ForceSuccess>

        <!-- 松开双爪 -->
        <ForceSuccess>
          <SetGripper arm_prefix="left" command="open" timeout_sec="3.0" />
        </ForceSuccess>
        <ForceSuccess>
          <SetGripper arm_prefix="right" command="open" timeout_sec="3.0" />
        </ForceSuccess>

        <!-- 清理 cube (无论 attach 在哪只手, 直接删掉) -->
        <ForceSuccess>
          <DetachObject object_name="cube"
              put_back_in_world="false"
              timeout_sec="3.0" />
        </ForceSuccess>

        <!-- 双臂回 home -->
        <ForceSuccess>
          <DualMoveToJointState named_pose="dual_home"
              velocity_scale="0.2" acceleration_scale="0.2"
              timeout_sec="20.0" />
        </ForceSuccess>

        <PrintMessage msg="=== RECOVERY DONE ==="/>
        <AlwaysFailure />
      </Sequence>

    </Fallback>
  </BehaviorTree>
</root>
```

### Launch 里加个 tree_file 参数

**M2.8.3 阶段先手动切换**——不改 launch 默认值。跑 handover 时:

```bash
ros2 param set /bt_executor tree_file pick_handover_place.xml
ros2 param set /bt_executor tree_id PickHandoverPlace
```

**不过**参数改了以后需要 executor 重新 `registerBehaviorTreeFromFile`,当前代码是启动时一次性 load 的。**要么重启 launch,要么让 executor 支持动态 reload**。

**简单起见**: 在 `s622_dual_arm.launch.py` 里加个 launch argument,启动时选:

```python
from launch.actions import DeclareLaunchArgument
from launch.substitutions import LaunchConfiguration

def generate_launch_description():
    # ... 前面所有代码不变 ...

    # ← 新增: 顶部加 tree 选择参数
    tree_file_arg = DeclareLaunchArgument(
        'tree_file', default_value='pick_place_dual.xml',
        description='BT XML file: pick_place_dual.xml | pick_handover_place.xml')
    tree_id_arg = DeclareLaunchArgument(
        'tree_id', default_value='PickPlaceDual',
        description='BT root ID matching tree_file')

    # ...

    # bt_executor 参数改成用 LaunchConfiguration
    bt_executor = TimerAction(
        period=22.0,
        actions=[
            Node(
                package="s622_bt_manager",
                executable="bt_executor_node",
                name="bt_executor",
                parameters=[
                    bt_dual_config,
                    {
                        "tree_file": LaunchConfiguration('tree_file'),  # ← 改
                        "tree_id":   LaunchConfiguration('tree_id'),    # ← 改
                        "tick_rate_hz": 10,
                        "auto_start": False,
                        "yolo_topic": "/yolov8/obb_detections",
                        "depth_topic": "/camera/depth/image_raw",
                        "caminfo_topic": "/camera/color/camera_info",
                        "grasp_viz_topic": "/grasp_visualization",
                        "arm": "left",
                        "use_sim_time": True,
                    },
                ],
                output="screen",
            )
        ],
    )

    return LaunchDescription([
        tree_file_arg,       # ← 新增
        tree_id_arg,         # ← 新增
        set_model_path,
        gazebo, clock_bridge, camera_bridge,
        # ... 其余不变 ...
    ])
```

### V4. 观察要点

**分阶段看**日志和 sim 画面:

| 阶段          | 期望日志                                                             | 视觉                                          |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| 起点          | `DualMoveToJointState [named=dual_home]` SUCCESS                     | 双臂到 home                                   |
| 左 pick       | MoveToPose left / VisualAlign left / SetGripper close / AttachObject | 左臂抓 cube                                   |
| 双臂 handover | `DualMoveToJointState [named=dual_handover_ready]` SUCCESS           | **两只手同步靠拢**,面对面 4cm 间距            |
| 右手咬        | `SetGripper[arm=right cmd=close]`                                    | 右手关闭,咬住 cube                            |
| Transfer      | `transferred cube -> right_grasp_frame`                              | RViz 里 cube 视觉上无变化,但 attach link 变了 |
| 左手松        | `SetGripper[arm=left cmd=open]`                                      | 左手打开,cube **不掉**                        |
| 双臂回 home   | `DualMoveToJointState [named=dual_home]`                             | 双臂让路,右臂带着 cube                        |
| 右 place      | MoveToPose right pre_place / place / SetGripper open / DetachObject  | 右臂放 cube                                   |
| 收尾          | `DualMoveToJointState [named=dual_home]`                             | 双臂回 home                                   |

**最终**: `[TRIAL_RESULT] status=SUCCESS`

## 可能遇到的坑

### 坑 A: dual_handover_ready 规划失败

若从 pick 完 lift 后的位姿 → dual_handover_ready 规划失败(路径穿桌面或自碰),先分段:

* 左臂先走单臂 `safe` (M2.7)
* 再 DualMoveToJointState 到 dual_handover_ready

### 坑 B: 右手 close 直接夹空

Handover 时 cube 在左手,右手位置若不完全对准 cube,close 后夹到空气。M2.8.1 你 teach 时是**双手空手**摆姿态,cube 到那位置的假设是**左臂 grasp_frame 位置 ≈ cube 位置**。

M2.8.1 你没验证过 cube attach 状态下能否到 handover(因为要先 pick)。若 V4 发现右手咬空,说明 teach 的姿态需要微调——把右手朝左手多推 1-2cm。

### 坑 C: 左手 open 后 cube 掉了

物理上 cube 会被右手夹住不掉。planning_scene 里 cube 已 attach 到 right_grasp_frame,视觉上也贴着右手。**但 gz sim 的物理**是根据右手夹爪 contact 决定的——如果 close 力度或位置不到位,cube 会掉。

如果掉了,右手 close 前加 `finger_position="{finger_pos}"` 反馈,或者调 gripper close 位置。

### 坑 D: TransferObject 顺序错

`AttachObject` 里代码有 `del self._attached[req.object_name]` 逻辑,但 `_on_attach` 里 attach 到 left 后 dict 里存的是 `left_grasp_frame`。若 M2.8.2 验证时你先 attach 到 left 又 attach 到 right(没走 transfer),dict 记录会不一致。

XML 里我用 `AttachObject arm_prefix="left"` → `TransferObject new_arm_prefix="right"` → `DetachObject arm_prefix="right"`,这个顺序 dict 状态是对的。放心。
